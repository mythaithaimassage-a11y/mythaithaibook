These terms apply to the MY THAI THAI booking webapp at mythaithaibook.vercel.app, operated by MY THAI THAI in Ontario, Canada. Please read them and our Privacy Policy before submitting a booking or medical-history form.

1. Booking and accurate information

Provide accurate contact, appointment, and guest information. Only submit information for yourself or someone you are authorized to assist. Appointment availability, services, prices, and therapist selection are shown in the booking flow and may change before a booking is submitted.

Keep your booking reference and use the email recorded on the booking for self-service features. If a confirmation email fails, the appointment may still have been saved. Check the on-screen status or contact the clinic before submitting a duplicate booking.

2. Prices and payment choices

The booking review shows the service amount, applicable taxes, discounts, and total. Available payment choices are configured per branch and may include payment at the clinic without an online deposit, a $10 online deposit (or the total if lower), or full online payment. When paying a deposit, the remaining balance is due at the clinic.

Online payments use Square's hosted checkout. Selecting an online payment option or receiving a booking reference is not proof that Square has processed a payment; complete checkout and retain the payment confirmation. Contact us if the displayed payment status does not match your payment record.

Membership benefits, prepaid packages, and company-sponsored plans depend on the applicable plan, available balance, and any required clinic verification. Selecting a company membership request does not itself activate a paid plan.

3. Cancellations and refunds

Cancel at least 24 hours before your appointment to qualify for a full refund of payments made for that appointment. Cancellation less than 24 hours before the appointment does not qualify for a refund under the clinic's booking policy. Applicable statutory rights remain unaffected.

Use the "Manage an existing booking" feature or contact the clinic. Eligible refunds may be processed through Square or require clinic assistance depending on the original payment method. Refund processing time depends on the payment provider and your financial institution.

4. Rescheduling

Rescheduling is free and subject to availability. Rescheduling does not issue a refund, regardless of timing; any recorded payment stays associated with the appointment. If a package-linked or otherwise restricted record cannot be changed through self-service, contact the clinic.

5. Medical history and treatment consent

Complete and accurate health information is required before treatment. You may skip medical history during online booking, but must complete it later through "Medical history only" using an existing booking reference and matching email, or at the clinic before treatment.

A standalone medical-history submission does not create an appointment or collect payment. Booking an appointment or skipping the form does not constitute medical-history or treatment consent. Treatment consent is requested separately, and you may ask questions or withdraw or modify treatment consent.

Tell your therapist about relevant conditions, medications, allergies, injuries, and changes in health. Massage and wellness services are not a substitute for medical diagnosis, emergency care, or advice from your healthcare provider.

6. Communications and privacy

We use the contact details you provide for appointment, payment, and applicable membership communications. Promotional email consent is optional and separate from booking and treatment consent. You may unsubscribe from promotions without cancelling an appointment. See our Privacy Policy for how information is handled.

7. Responsible use

Do not use the webapp to submit false or unauthorized bookings, access another person's records without permission, misuse private portal links, interfere with the service, or attempt to bypass access controls. Keep staff credentials and private self-service links confidential.

8. Availability and issues

The app relies on hosting, Calendar, email, and payment services that may occasionally be unavailable. If a booking, history submission, payment, cancellation, or refund appears unsuccessful or inconsistent, contact us rather than relying on an error message alone or submitting repeated payments.

Nothing in these terms excludes rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded. These terms do not replace a separate treatment-consent process or professional obligations applicable to your treatment.
