Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy describes how MY THAI THAI ("we", "us", or "our") handles information through its booking webapp at mythaithaibook.vercel.app. It covers appointment booking and management, medical-history submissions, payments, and optional membership and marketing features.

For privacy questions or requests, contact mythaithaimassage@gmail.com. Please do not send detailed medical history, payment-card information, passwords, or access tokens in an ordinary email.

1. Information collected

  • Booking and contact details: name, email, phone number, booking reference, selected branch, service, therapist, appointment date and time, and a second guest's name when booking a couple session.
  • Medical history and treatment preferences: information you choose to submit about date of birth, gender, address, medical conditions, medications, allergies, pain or affected body areas, pressure preferences, treatment notes, and consent and signature details.
  • Payment and receipt records: appointment amounts, payment status, payment-provider transaction references, refunds, and receipts. Online card entry is handled through Square's hosted checkout, not a card-entry form in this webapp.
  • Optional membership information: enrollment, rewards, prepaid hours or packages, and company or employee details where applicable.
  • Preferences and technical information: marketing opt-in and unsubscribe status, consent timestamps, sign-in/session information for staff, and technical request or error information processed by hosting and infrastructure services.

2. How information is used

We use information to schedule and manage appointments, communicate booking details, support safe treatment, keep patient and business records, process payments and refunds, issue receipts, operate memberships, and respond to support and privacy requests.

You can skip medical history during booking and submit it later using your booking reference and email, or complete it at the clinic before treatment. Skipping the form does not give treatment consent or create a completed medical-history record. Marketing consent is separate and optional; declining it does not prevent booking.

3. Google services and Gmail authorization

The webapp uses Google Cloud BigQuery for booking, patient-history, payment, and related business records, and Google Sheets for configuration and certain operational records. Google Calendar is used to schedule appointments. Calendar entries can contain booking/contact details, service, therapist, payment summary, and supplied intake notes. They do not contain the full medical-history questionnaire by default.

The clinic's sender-mailbox owner authorizes Gmail using the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send permission. This allows the webapp to send confirmation emails, receipts, membership notices, and authorized customer communications from that mailbox. Customers do not need to grant access to their own Gmail accounts to book. This integration does not request permission to read customers' inboxes.

The webapp's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google API data is used for the features described here, not sold or used for personalized advertising or to train generalized AI models. The sender-mailbox owner can revoke authorization through Google Account third-party connections; doing so stops the app's ability to send Gmail messages until reauthorized.

4. Service providers and authorized access

Information is processed by service providers needed to run the webapp, including Vercel for hosting, Google for cloud storage, spreadsheets, Calendar and Gmail, and Square for online payments. Provider processing is subject to their applicable terms and privacy policies. Processing or storage may occur outside your province or Canada.

Authorized clinic staff access information for their work. Therapist views provide appointment and patient information relevant to treatment. Where you join a company-sponsored membership, the designated company contact may receive membership notifications and usage information associated with that plan. The company portal does not provide the full medical-history questionnaire.

We may disclose information with your authorization, as required by applicable law, or where legally permitted to protect people, systems, or legal rights. This webapp does not sell personal information.

5. Optional AI-assisted marketing tools

Staff may use Google Gemini to help draft campaign text or interpret audience filters. These requests use staff-entered campaign goals or audience descriptions and service, branch, membership, or aggregate audience information. The app does not automatically include patient medical histories, customer names, customer email addresses, or individual booking rows in those requests. Staff must avoid putting personal or medical information into free-text AI prompts. Customer records are not sent to Gemini to select individual recipients; that matching happens within the app's own data processing.

6. Marketing communications

Promotional emails require the separate marketing opt-in offered in the booking form. You can unsubscribe using the link in a promotional email or contact us. Unsubscribing from marketing does not prevent necessary appointment, payment, or membership communications. Marketing and membership choices are separate.

7. Cookies, browser storage, and security

The app uses session cookies for owner and therapist sign-in, and browser storage for features such as staff campaign drafts and saved app configuration. Hosting and payment providers may also process technical information needed for their services. Protect your booking reference, matching email details, and private company-portal links because they are used to access self-service features.

Access controls and server-side credentials help protect records, but no online system can guarantee absolute security. Please use a trusted device and avoid sharing confidential information beyond what is necessary for your appointment.

8. Retention, access, and correction

Records are retained as needed for treatment, appointment administration, accounting, membership management, dispute resolution, and applicable legal and professional recordkeeping requirements. Medical or financial records may need to be retained even after you stop using the app. This policy does not promise immediate deletion or a single retention period for every record type.

Contact us to request access to or correction of your information, discuss retention or deletion, or withdraw a consent where applicable. We may need to verify your identity. Requests are subject to applicable law, professional obligations, and lawful retention requirements. Withdrawing consent does not undo processing already lawfully performed and may affect features that need that information.

9. Changes and contact

Updates will be published on this page with a revised date. For questions about this policy, contact MY THAI THAI at mythaithaimassage@gmail.com or +1 437 898 7424.
